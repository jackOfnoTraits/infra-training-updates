# Day 20 – Business Insights and Recommendations

**Objective:** Analyze the e-commerce dataset to identify key business trends and recommend actionable improvements.

### 1. Sales Performance

- **Insight:** Fashion contributes the highest revenue (35.91%), followed by Home & Kitchen (26.99%).
- **Interpretation:** Revenue is concentrated in a few product categories.
- **Recommendation:** Prioritize high-performing categories while using targeted promotions to improve sales in weaker categories.

### 2. Customer Trends

- **Insight:** 500 out of 774 customers (64.6%) made repeat purchases.
- **Interpretation:** The business demonstrates good customer retention potential.
- **Recommendation:** Introduce loyalty programmes, personalized offers and repeat-purchase incentives.

### 3. Order Patterns

- **Insight:** The business recorded 1,650 orders with an average order value of approximately Rs. 1,499. Around 38.6% of orders contain multiple product lines.
- **Interpretation:** There is an opportunity to increase the number of products purchased per transaction.
- **Recommendation:** Implement product bundling, cross-selling and complementary product recommendations.

### 4. Profitability

- **Insight:** Overall gross profit margin is 52.31%. Electronics generates the highest category margin at approximately 67.06%.
- **Interpretation:** Electronics offers stronger profitability despite Fashion generating higher revenue.
- **Recommendation:** Focus on high-margin products and review discount strategies for lower-margin categories.

### 5. Sales Target Achievement

- **Insight:** Actual sales are approximately Rs. 2.47M against dataset-defined targets of Rs. 2.64M, achieving approximately 93.65%.
- **Interpretation:** Sales performance is reasonably close to the expected target, but there is still a revenue gap.
- **Recommendation:** Monitor monthly and city-level performance, investigate underperforming segments and introduce targeted sales campaigns.

### Conclusion

The analysis indicates strong revenue contribution from Fashion, encouraging customer retention and healthy overall profitability. The business can improve its performance through customer retention initiatives, increased basket value, better discount management and focused sales strategies to close the target achievement gap.
