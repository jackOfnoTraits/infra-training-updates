## Description

Linux Under the Microscope — 10 Uncommon Commands


# | Command | What makes it interesting | Practical challenge
1 | chrt | Inspect and configure real-time process scheduling policies. | Compare normal and real-time scheduling priorities.
2 | renice | Change the CPU scheduling priority of running processes. | Adjust the priority of a CPU-intensive process.
3 | prlimit | Inspect and modify resource limits for processes. | Inspect file descriptor and memory limits.
4 | namei | Trace every component of a pathname and its permissions. | Diagnose why a user cannot access a directory.
5 | getfacl | Inspect detailed filesystem access-control lists. | Investigate inherited and user-specific permissions.
6 | setfacl | Configure fine-grained filesystem permissions. | Grant one user access without changing the file owner or group.
7 | flock | Implement file-based locking to coordinate processes. | Prevent two scripts from running the same job simultaneously.
8 | socat | Create connections between sockets, files, and network endpoints. | Build a simple TCP relay between two local ports.
9 | openssl | Inspect certificates, test TLS connections, and perform cryptographic operations. | Inspect a website's TLS certificate.
10 | strace | Trace system calls and signals made by a process. | Investigate why a program fails to open a file.
