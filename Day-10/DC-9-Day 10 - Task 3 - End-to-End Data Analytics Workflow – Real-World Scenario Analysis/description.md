## Description

Select one real-world business scenario. Perform an end-to-end data analytics workflow, starting from raw data collection and ending with actionable business decisions.

At each stage, document the steps performed, tools used, techniques applied, and outcomes obtained.

Task Requirements:

1. Scenario Selection: Select one real-world scenario and define the business problem, objectives, and key questions to be answered.
2. Raw Data Collection: Identify the required dataset and its source. Explain the data fields, data format, and relevance to the business problem.
3. Data Loading: Load the raw dataset into the selected tool or environment. Explain the loading method and tools used.
4. Data Exploration (EDA): Understand the dataset's structure, data types, distributions, patterns, and relationships. Identify initial trends and anomalies.
5. Data Cleaning and Preprocessing: Handle missing values, duplicate records, inconsistent formats, incorrect data types, and outliers where appropriate. Document the techniques applied.
6. Data Analysis: Perform relevant calculations, aggregations, comparisons, trend analysis, and statistical analysis to answer the business questions.
7. Insights Generation: Identify meaningful patterns, trends, problems, and opportunities from the analysis. Support the insights with data, charts, or summary tables.
8. Business Decisions and Recommendations: Convert the insights into actionable recommendations. Explain how the recommendations can help improve business performance.
9. Tools and Techniques Documentation: For every stage, specify the tools used, techniques applied, purpose, and output generated.
10. Final Presentation: Prepare a structured report or presentation containing the scenario, dataset, workflow, tools, techniques, analysis, visualizations, insights, and business recommendations. Expected Deliverable:
A complete end-to-end data analytics project with documented workflow stages, tools and techniques, analysis results, visualizations, actionable insights, and business recommendations.

Acceptance Criteria:

- One real-world scenario and business problem are clearly defined.
- A relevant dataset is selected and loaded successfully.
- Data exploration and cleaning are completed and documented.
- Appropriate analytical techniques are applied.
- Each workflow stage includes the tools, techniques, and outputs.
- Insights are supported by data and visualizations.
- Business decisions and recommendations are derived from the findings.
- The final report or presentation is submitted for review.
